The Ritual · 04

Five acts. One afternoon.

Our standard session runs two to four hours depending on service. Every stage is designed to feel unhurried — because the work is better when the person in the chair is.

A MAISON Afternoon
2–4hrs

Including consultation, espresso, treatment, craft, and finish. Arrive early, leave reluctantly.

01
10 min · arrival

Arrival & espresso

You're greeted by name, handed an espresso or mint tea, shown to your chair — and given the space to settle in without being rushed into a cape.

02
15 min · consultation

The consultation

A real conversation. Your stylist reads your hair dry, asks about your mornings, your mirrors, and the three photos you saved last month on your phone.

03
60–180 min · craft

The craft

Cut, colour, or both — unhurried, unmedalled with checkpoints. You'll be asked for input; you won't be asked to wait.

04
20 min · ritual

The ritual wash

Warm towel, scalp massage, our house conditioning mask — five minutes of quiet you'll remember for weeks.

05
20 min · finish

Finish & farewell

Hand-dried and styled so you can recreate it. We take a portfolio photo (optional), book your follow-up at 8 or 10 weeks, and send you out into whatever comes next.

How do I choose the right stylist?

Start anywhere. Every stylist at MAISON is trained to the same house standard, and if we feel you'd be better matched elsewhere after your consultation, we'll tell you — and rebook you, on us. Most guests find their person within the first two visits and stay for years.

Do you accept walk-ins?

We don't — but we keep a same-day cancellation list. Text us in the morning with what you're hoping for and we'll call if a chair opens up. It happens more often than you'd think, especially mid-week.

What's your cancellation policy?

A deposit of 50% holds your chair. Cancel more than 48 hours out, it's fully refundable. Inside 48 hours, the deposit covers the stylist's time. Inside 24, the full service. We keep it strict because our time blocks are — and because no-shows hurt the people on the waitlist.

How far in advance should I book?

Three to five weeks for a new guest, six to eight for a returning one — longer for bridal (six months is not unreasonable). Our members get a 48-hour head-start on every release.

Do you work with curly and textured hair?

Yes — and well. Saskia specialises in 3a–4c curl patterns and locs, and the rest of our team trains with her quarterly. We cut textured hair dry, never wet, and we don't charge extra for length or density.

Is there parking nearby?

Metered street parking on Kearny (plentiful after 6pm and Sundays), a lot on NW Lovejoy a block over, and MAX-light-rail stops two minutes away. We reimburse parking for services over $200.
